Bug LH-56: the occupancy report made hotels look fuller ✔ Fixed

How full the hotels were in the week of 1–7 Oct 2026. Hover over a bar for the exact numbers.

What the report said (wrong)Real count from the night audit

Why it happened

A guest who arrives on Thursday and leaves on Friday morning stays 1 night. The old report counted every day from arrival up to and including the checkout day, so it counted 2 nights. The fix counts up to but not including the checkout day. In code, the change was one character: day <= checkout became day < checkout.

⚠ There is still no automatic check proving the fix stays fixed (open bug G).
